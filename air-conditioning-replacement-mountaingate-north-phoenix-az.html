Project overview

  • Location: Desert Peak / Mountain Gate community, North Phoenix, AZ 85024
  • Project Type: Residential Air Conditioning Replacement (Retrofit)
  • Timeline: Same-Day Installation (12 hours)
  • Investment: $19,000

The challenge

In the upscale Mountaingate community near Cave Creek Road and Pinnacle Peak Road, our client faced a common yet frustrating scenario for Arizona homeowners. After purchasing their home, they quickly discovered the existing HVAC system couldn't keep pace with Phoenix's demanding summer temperatures.

Our assessment revealed:

report_problemThe existing 12 SEER split gas system was inefficient and outdated
report_problemThe home experienced uncomfortable temperature fluctuations during peak heat
report_problemThe system struggled to maintain consistent cooling throughout the property
report_problemThe homeowner couldn't comfortably remain in the home during afternoon hours
report_problemSleep quality was compromised due to inadequate nighttime cooling
report_problemThe owner worried about their pets' safety when away from home

While the homeowner had purchased the property knowing the system would eventually need replacement, the inadequate performance during Arizona's intense summer heat accelerated the timeline, making replacement an immediate priority rather than a future consideration.

Our solution

After a thorough evaluation of the property's specific needs, we recommended installing a premium Coleman Echelon™ Variable-Speed 20 SEER split gas system. This recommendation wasn't just about replacing equipment — it was about providing a comprehensive solution to transform the home's comfort levels while delivering exceptional energy efficiency.

Our proposed solution included:

check_circleA Coleman Echelon™ HC20 Variable-Speed 20 SEER split gas system
check_circlePremium installation focused on Comfort One's “day one quality” philosophy
check_circleComprehensive 10-year parts and 10-year labor warranty protection
check_circleSystem properly sized and configured for the specific home requirements
check_circleOptimization for Arizona's extreme climate conditions

The implementation

Our experienced team completed the entire system replacement in just 12 hours, showcasing our commitment to minimizing disruption while maintaining meticulous installation standards.

The installation process included:

check_circleCareful removal of the old 12 SEER system
check_circlePrecise installation of the new Coleman variable-speed system
check_circleComprehensive testing and calibration
check_circleDetailed system walkthrough with the homeowner
check_circleFinal quality inspections adhering to our rigorous standards

As a family-owned company serving North Scottsdale and surrounding communities, we pride ourselves on “getting it right the first time.” This installation exemplified our core philosophy that the most important day in any HVAC system's life is the day it's installed.

The results

The new Coleman variable-speed system delivered immediate and dramatic improvements to the homeowner's quality of life:

check_circleSuperior Comfort: The home now maintains consistent temperatures throughout, eliminating hot and cold spots
check_circleSignificant Energy Savings: The homeowner will see between 35-50% savings on their cooling-related energy bills
check_circleImproved Sleep Quality: The homeowner reported being able to “finally sleep through the night” with optimal temperature control
check_circleDaytime Comfort: The home remains comfortable even during peak afternoon heat, allowing the homeowner to stay home when previously impossible
check_circlePeace of Mind for Pets: The reliable system ensures pets remain safe and comfortable when the homeowner is away
check_circleLong-Term Protection: Comprehensive 10-year parts and 10-year labor warranty provides lasting security and value

Why this matters

This Mountaingate community project represents a common scenario throughout North Phoenix and Scottsdale areas, where homeowners with aging or inefficient HVAC systems struggle with Arizona's extreme temperatures.

The transformation from an inadequate 12 SEER system to a premium Coleman 20 SEER variable-speed solution demonstrates how professional installation and quality equipment can dramatically improve quality of life while delivering substantial energy savings.

At Comfort One Air Conditioning, we believe that your HVAC system should be invisible — quietly maintaining perfect comfort without drawing attention to itself. This project embodies our commitment to creating homes where comfort is a given, not a constant concern.

For homeowners in luxury communities throughout North Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Cave Creek, and surrounding areas, this case study illustrates the difference that proper system selection and expert installation make in creating truly comfortable homes in Arizona's challenging climate.
