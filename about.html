About our family owned & operated HVAC company

Comfort One Air Conditioning is a family owned & operated HVAC company based in Scottsdale, AZ. Founded in 2012, Comfort One Air Conditioning was created to fill the need for exceptional customer service in the HVAC industry.

We pride ourselves on the culture we have created that implements our values of family, integrity, dedication to excellence and a growth mindset while providing professional and expert level HVAC services in Scottsdale, AZ and surrounding areas.

Over 2,750 customers get to feel this differentiator when interacting with any of our team members. Call us today and let us be the last HVAC company you will ever need!

"I know for a fact that we can build a HVAC company that focuses on our customers first. We strive to build value, no matter if it is just a routine maintenance, a repair job, replacement or new installation. Nowadays, nobody likes to call the HVAC guys over to their home because of the discomfort of being upsold into things that are not needed. It is our mission to build a company that our customers love to call. Because in the end of the day, we are a customer service company that maintains, repairs and installs HVAC systems."

— Tapani Ojalehto, Founder

A message
from the owners…

Comfort One Air Conditioning was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to provide unparalleled HVAC service that truly puts the customer first. Jessica and my journey began when we noticed a troubling trend in the industry — late arrivals, unprofessional behavior, and a lack of genuine care for customers' comfort and time. Upsells wherever you look. Units not installed properly.

We knew we could do better. That's why we founded Comfort One with a clear mission: to deliver excellence through exceptional customer service and top-notch workmanship, all while maintaining fair prices and nurturing great relationships with both our customers and employees.

Our main goals include:

  • Creating a positive and growth oriented workplace that is rooted in family
  • Becoming North Scottsdale's most trusted and reliable HVAC company
  • Maintaining fair, reasonable, and transparent pricing

With four decades of experience, over 2,500 HVAC systems repaired, and more than 2,750 satisfied customers, we're confident in our slogan: "We Are Here For You. On Time, Every Time. Your Comfort, Our Priority."
