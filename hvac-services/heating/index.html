Frequently asked questions

We recommend servicing your heating system once a year, typically in the fall before cooler weather sets in. Regular maintenance helps prevent breakdowns, improves efficiency, and extends the life of your system.
Our technicians inspect and clean all components, check and tighten electrical connections, lubricate moving parts, test thermostat operation, check airflow, inspect the heat exchanger (for furnaces), test safety controls, and look for any signs of wear.
With proper maintenance, most heating systems last 15–20 years. This varies by system type, usage, and climate. In Scottsdale's milder climate, systems may last longer due to less intense use than in colder regions.
Banging or popping can be expanding/contracting ductwork; whistling may indicate air leaks; scraping could be a blower-wheel problem; clicking might be a failing ignitor or control board. It's best to have a professional inspect it to prevent damage.
Regularly change or clean air filters, use a programmable thermostat, ensure proper insulation, keep vents and registers unobstructed, schedule annual maintenance, and consider upgrading if your system is old.
